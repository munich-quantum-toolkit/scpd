⚡ Narrow the refinement band and make the Final stage's searches cheaper

The capacity rule of the coupler insertion, the profile cuts and the
bottleneck report are on by default (SCPD_CAPACITY_RULE,
SCPD_CAPACITY_FAST=1, SCPD_BOTTLENECKS). With them every benchmark ends
the feedline routing with no bad wire.

Both refinements now search a band of their own,
refinement_corridor_spacings: 6 wire spacings by default, 0 takes
corridor_spacings. A refinement moves a way that already stands, and its
search, its corridor and its room price grow with the band. Fewer rounds
were measured as well and rejected: 45q and 69q end with bad wires.

Three changes give the same artifacts byte for byte on all eight
benchmarks:

- DubinsRouter::attachCorridor packs again only the 64-cell words of the
  corridor that changed since the last packing.
- The room price of the refinement is computed inside the box of the
  corridor's open cells, grown by one cell.
- The free strip of a meander along an axis reads the free depth of each
  cell once per insertion (DubinsRouter::FreeStripMemo).

To the end of the feedline refinement, one chip at a time on the Mac
(whole run, before -> after; bad / short / long after):
