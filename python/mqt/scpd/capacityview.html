Capacity graph after the coupler insertion

Chip
Graph: chambers, edges with load/capacity, ports as leaves

Per feedline edge: the stretches a wire may cross it on and the bottlenecks that end at it. Spare is the least of capacity − load over all of them.

A partition is a chamber of the graph: free space that no bottleneck cuts. Every terminal lies in the slot of its port run, and so in one partition.

Pick a coupler in the list or click its pad on the chip: every option it has is drawn — blue the ones the chain search offers, dashed the ones it does not. A click on an option, on the chip or in the list below, puts the coupler on it; the server draws its two feedline edges again and analyses the chip anew.
